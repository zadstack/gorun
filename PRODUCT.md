# GoRun product context

## Register
product

## Users
Independent site owners, editors and small teams creating a first site, publishing Arabic or English content and managing scoped agents. Existing sites and content must remain intact during design changes.

## Product Purpose
A self-hostable Go and SQLite CMS with a native admin, editable themes and starter sites. A new owner chooses a design, supplies identity and content, previews drafts, then explicitly publishes. See README.md and DESIGN.md.

## Brand Personality
Clear, cooperative, deliberate. Prefer useful, factual controls and readable content over decoration.

## Anti-references
Dark default admin, oversized hero sections, crowded technical metadata, unnecessary animation and identical generic marketing cards.

## Design Principles
- Light, compact administrative workspace with obvious selected and focus states.
- Public templates emphasize actual content and retain distinct site identities.
- Preview and publishing remain separate explicit actions.
- Preserve existing user data, theme settings and permissions.
- Native Go templates and CSS; self-hosted Three.js is an optional progressive enhancement for homepage hero presets.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Arabic-first with full RTL and English support; readable Arabic line heights, keyboard focus, responsive layouts, reduced motion and WCAG AA contrast as the review target.
